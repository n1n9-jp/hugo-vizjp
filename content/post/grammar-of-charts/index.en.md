The same points form different shapes on screen depending on whether you place them in Cartesian or polar coordinates. The Grammar of Charts lets you separate foreground marks, such as points and lines, from their background coordinate system, then put the two back together.

**[Open The Grammar of Charts](https://grammar-of-charts.visualizing.jp/)** (the interactive is in Japanese).

<!--more-->

## Change the background and keep the points

Use the left and right arrow keys to reach “背景をえらぶ” (choose a background), then use the up and down arrows to select a coordinate system. Compare where the same points appear on Cartesian, polar, geographic and network backgrounds. In “かさねる” (overlay), you can examine the foreground and background of nine charts.

[About the interactive and its data sources](https://github.com/visualizing-jp/grammar-of-charts)
