## Theoretical Approach

I approach data visualization as a design discipline rather than a matter of intuition. My work draws on Bertin’s visual variables, Cleveland and McGill’s research on perceptual accuracy, Mackinlay’s APT, and Wilkinson’s *Grammar of Graphics*.

Building on these foundations, I have developed two frameworks through professional practice and teaching.

**Chart Grammar** — A way of understanding charts as layered structures composed of rules for the drawing space and the graphical representation of data. It condenses Wilkinson’s system into four practical elements: visual transformation, spatial transformation, non-data graphics, and scale.

**The 5W Classification for Charts** — A framework that classifies charts not by task, but by the lens through which data is visualized: quantity, words, time, place, and relationships. This approach includes text, maps, and networks—forms often overlooked by conventional task-centered classifications.

Both frameworks are presented in *データ可視化の基本が全部わかる本* and have been used in corporate training and university courses for more than 420 participants.

### Long Bio
Yuichi Yazaki is a data visualization specialist and Representative Partner of Notation LLC.

After seven years as a user interface designer and art director at Business Architects Inc., he became independent. He specializes in designing ways to transform data into understandable forms, working across dashboard design, information design, corporate training, university education, writing, and public engagement.

His training experience includes programs for news organizations and major corporations, including NHK (500 employees) and Yomiuri Shimbun (five executive-training sessions for 350 participants). He teaches as an adjunct lecturer at Tama Art University, Musashino Art University, Digital Hollywood University Graduate School, Nihon University, Tokyo City University, and Bunkyo University. His extensive speaking experience in Japan and abroad includes presentations in the United States, Germany, Lithuania, and Thailand. Visualizing.JP, which he has operated since 2013, is one of the longest-running Japanese-language sources dedicated to data visualization.

He is the author of *データ可視化の基本が全部わかる本* (Shoeisha, 2024) and co-author of *RESASの教科書* (Nikkei BP, 2016). He founded Data Visualization Japan, contributes as a Yahoo! News Expert, and holds a master’s degree in Design Science from the Graduate School of Chiba Institute of Technology.
